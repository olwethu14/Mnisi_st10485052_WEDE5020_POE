Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive my CV?

Your CV will be completed within the agreed turnaround time after all required information has been received.

Yes. Our CV Editing service improves the wording, structure and presentation of your existing CV.

Do you offer cover letters?

Yes. We provide personalised cover letters designed to complement your CV.

Do you offer revisions?

Revision options can be discussed depending on the service selected.

Are my documents ATS-friendly?

Yes. Our professional CVs are structured to be clear, professional and suitable for Applicant Tracking Systems.

You will need to provide relevant information about your education, work experience, skills and career goals.
